<b>Atril PDF — your digital music stand.</b> Made by a musician for every musician.

Carry all your scores on your tablet, phone or computer: no more photocopies, clips or falling pages. For students, teachers, conservatories, bands, orchestras and choirs.

<b>Truly free:</b> no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions, no purchases and no data collection. Works offline, on stage too.

<b>MADE FOR PLAYING</b>
• Instant page turns: tap, swipe, keyboard or a <b>Bluetooth pedal</b>, with no setup.
• Smooth, quick page animation, a fade, or none at all.
• <b>Half-page turns</b> like a real stand: see the top of the next page while you finish the current one.
• Vertical with look-ahead, continuous, or <b>two pages</b> like a book.
• <b>Pinch to zoom</b> (or Ctrl + wheel on a computer) to read small passages perfectly sharp.
• Full-screen performance mode that keeps the screen on.
• Night mode, sepia and margin cropping for bigger staves.

<b>CONCERTS</b>
• A folder for each concert or repertoire: <b>drag</b> your pieces in and reorder them by dragging.
• <b>Start concert</b>: all pieces in a row; when one ends, the next one opens.

<b>WRITE ON YOUR SCORE</b>
• Pen, highlighter, eraser and text. Works with Apple Pencil and styluses, with a "pencil only" option so your palm never draws.
• A five-line <b>staff</b> to add bars or corrections, at any size.
• Place <b>images</b> on the page, move and resize them.
• Your notes never change the original PDF and can always be undone.
• Share the score as a PDF, with or without your notes: by email, WhatsApp, AirDrop… or save it on your device.

<b>ORGANISE PAGES</b>
• A panel with every page numbered: reorder, duplicate, delete, add blank pages, another PDF or an image.

<b>LIBRARY</b>
• Import PDFs and images, search, favourites, most recent and a recoverable trash.

<b>IN YOUR LANGUAGE</b>
• 21 languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Greek, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. It follows your device language.
